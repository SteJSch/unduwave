standard orientation for interpretation of the variable names is in (0,1,0) direction, so the coils lying in the x-z plane.

coil_len_x - extent of coil in x-direction coil_thick - thickness of coil in x-z-plane outer_z - outer length of coil in z-direction inner_z - inner length of coil in z-direction inner_r - the inner curvature of the coils at the edges, the higher, the more circle-like height - the extent in y-direction segm_v - segmentations segm_h=10 segm_r=10 filling-the percentage of coil querschnitt filled by coils wire_diameter - diameter of one wire n_windings=int(height*coil_thick/(math.pi*wire_diameter**2))
